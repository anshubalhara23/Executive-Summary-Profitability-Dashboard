# 📊 ABC Executive Summary & Profitability Dashboard | Excel

## 📌 Project Overview

The **ABC Executive Summary and Profitability Dashboard** is an interactive Microsoft Excel dashboard designed to analyze business performance, sales trends, and profitability across different product categories, countries, regions, and sales channels.

The dashboard transforms business data into meaningful visual insights through KPI cards, Pivot Tables, interactive slicers, and multiple data visualizations.

It provides an executive-level overview of key business metrics, helping users monitor revenue, evaluate profitability, compare sales channels, and explore geographical performance.

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies

- **Microsoft Excel**
- Pivot Tables
- Pivot Charts
- Interactive Slicers
- KPI Cards
- Data Visualization
- Geographical Map Visualization
- Business & Profitability Analysis

## 📈 Dashboard Overview

### 1. Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

The dashboard provides six key business metrics:

| KPI | Value |
|---|---:|
| Total Revenue | $596,889 |
| Total Gross Profit | $272,456 |
| Gross Profit Margin | 45.65% |
| Total Units Sold | 5,925 |
| Total Orders | 2,000 |
| Cost of Goods Sold (COGS) | $324,433 |

### 2. Monthly Revenue & Profit Analysis

- Tracks monthly revenue and gross profit trends.
- Compares revenue against gross profit.
- Identifies monthly fluctuations in business performance.

### 3. Top 5 Countries by Revenue

- Highlights the five highest-revenue countries.
- Enables geographical comparison of sales performance.

### 4. Channel-wise Sales Analysis

Analyzes sales distribution across four business channels:

- Corporate
- Marketplace
- Online Store
- Retail Partners

### 5. Region-wise Sales Analysis

Visualizes sales distribution across four geographical regions:

- Central Europe
- Northern Europe
- Southern Europe
- Western Europe

### 6. Geographical Revenue Analysis

- Displays revenue distribution across countries using a geographical map.
- Helps identify geographical sales concentration and market performance.

## 🎛️ Interactive Dashboard Features

The dashboard includes three interactive slicers:

- **Product Category:** Camping, Cycling, Fitness, Hiking, and Travel.
- **Country:** Filter business performance by individual countries.
- **Quarter:** Analyze performance across Q1, Q2, Q3, and Q4.

These slicers allow users to dynamically filter dashboard visualizations and explore business performance from different perspectives.

## 🔄 Project Workflow

1. Prepared and organized the business dataset.
2. Used Excel Pivot Tables to summarize business metrics.
3. Developed KPI cards to display important performance indicators.
4. Created charts to analyze revenue, profitability, sales channels, and geographical performance.
5. Integrated interactive slicers for dynamic filtering.
6. Designed an executive summary dashboard to present business insights in a single view.

## 💡 Key Insights

The dashboard provides the ability to:

- Monitor overall revenue and profitability.
- Compare monthly revenue and gross profit trends.
- Identify top-performing countries by revenue.
- Evaluate sales contribution across different channels.
- Compare regional sales distribution.
- Explore business performance by product category, country, and quarter.
